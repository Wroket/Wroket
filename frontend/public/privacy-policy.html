Privacy contact: team@wroket.com.

Google user data we access

When you sign in with Google and/or connect Google Calendar, Wroket accesses the following Google user data with your consent:

  • Google Account profile (openid, email, profile): Google user ID, email address, name, and profile picture — used only to create and authenticate your Wroket account.
  • Google Calendar read (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly): event titles, start/end times, durations, and free/busy status for calendars you explicitly select — used to show busy slots in Wroket Agenda and suggest free slots for tasks.
  • Google Calendar write (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events): create or update calendar events and optional Google Meet links — used only when you explicitly book or sync a Wroket time slot. Connecting OAuth alone does not write any events.

How we use Google user data

Wroket uses Google user data solely to provide and improve user-facing features that are prominent in the product (account sign-in, Agenda free/busy display, and explicit calendar booking/sync). We do not use Google user data for advertising, analytics resale, credit decisions, lending, or any purpose other than operating these features.

AI / ML affirmation: Google user data obtained via Google APIs (including Google Calendar / Workspace-related scopes) is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

How we share, transfer, or disclose Google user data

We do not sell Google user data. We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties for purposes other than providing or improving Wroket, except:

  • Google Cloud Platform as hosting sub-processor (EU regions) to run the application infrastructure;
  • when required by applicable law.

Optional in-app features that send a photo or note text you choose (Cloud Vision / Gemini) do not receive Google Calendar OAuth data. Those features only process content you explicitly submit for that action.

How we store and protect Google user data

  • Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data.
  • We use encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and encryption at rest for sensitive data, including Google OAuth tokens.
  • Access to production data is limited to application service accounts required to operate the service.

Data retention and deletion

  • Account data: kept while your account is active; permanently deleted after you request account deletion.
  • Google Calendar data: not retained as a long-term copy; short-lived cache only for Agenda display. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and revoked when you disconnect Google Calendar in Wroket or revoke access in your Google Account.
  • Deletion / export: request at team@wroket.com or via in-app account data controls.
  • Technical logs: at most 90 days. Backups: at most 30 days.

Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Wroket's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data we collect (non-Google)

  • Account fields you enter and application data you create (tasks, projects, notes).
  • Strictly necessary cookies (session, language, theme). No advertising cookies.

Your rights (GDPR)

You may access, rectify, delete, export, object to, or restrict processing of your data, and withdraw consent (including Google access) at any time. Contact team@wroket.com. You may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (France) or your local EU supervisory authority.
